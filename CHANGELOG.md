# Changelog

## 0.1.0

You can now keep a notch on the top edge of the screen, with one lamp for each coding agent on this machine.

You can now read grey as closed, yellow as open and idle, green as a task executing, and red as a task waiting on you.

You can now open an agent's own window, maximised, by clicking its row. Codex and the ChatGPT desktop app share one lamp.

You can now choose which agents are watched, and the language and theme, from the gear. That choice stays in Fanal's own settings file.

You can now leave the notch collapsed without it covering the browser tabs underneath. It closes when the pointer leaves, and when you click somewhere else.
